# Experimental JavaCard Applets

The native ECC and PACE probes are independent research applets.

- Native ECC: BabyJubJub-compatible Weierstrass multiplication
- PACE GM: native public-point addition with scalar s=1

Imported CAP files are fingerprint-verified against physical
JCOP3 test artifacts.

A compiled CAP does not establish JCOP4 or JCOP4.5 compatibility.
Installing applets may modify card storage and must be explicitly
authorized.

Sources imported from the original experiments may depend
on separate JavaCard SDKs and build-time libraries.
