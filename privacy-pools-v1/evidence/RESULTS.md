# Privacy Pools V1 — Research Evidence

- Physical Keycard signing and recovery investigated
- Deterministic BIP-85-derived credentials validated
- Keycard Shell encrypted QR credential exchange validated
- Sepolia Privacy Pools deposit and withdrawal demonstrated
- Groth16 verification and remaining-note recovery examined

## Critical limitation

Sensitive proving material was available to the host.
A transaction signed by hardware does not imply that the
privacy credentials or proof generation were hardware-isolated.

See historical V3/V4 reports for exact transaction identifiers,
test conditions, and checkpoint evidence.
